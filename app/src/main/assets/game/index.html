HOW TO PLAY

🛴 STEERING: Drag the joystick on the bottom-left to steer across the bridge.

🚀 JUMP: Tap the green JUMP button to leap over crates, jump barriers, and gaps.

🛑 BRAKE: Tap the red BRAKE button to reduce forward speed by up to 25% for tight maneuvers.

🟡 COINS & MULTIPLIER: Collect gold coins! Every 10 consecutive coins increases your multiplier (up to x5).

💎 GEMS: Purple crystals award +100 bonus points each!

⚡ POWER-UPS: Shield (absorbs 1 hit), Magnet (pulls coins), 2X Coins, Heart (+1 heart), Overdrive (speed surge)!

🏆 CAMPAIGN: Beat all 16 rounds to unlock Endless Mode!

⌨️ DESKTOP: A/D or Left/Right to steer, Space to jump, S to brake, Esc to pause.
