HOW TO PLAY

MOVE Touch the left side and drag: left/right steers, up accelerates, down brakes. The stick appears under your thumb.

JUMP Tap anywhere on the right side. Hold in the air to glide while antigravity is active.

BRAKE Tap and hold BRAKE for tight maneuvers.

OBSTACLES A hit, including landing on top, costs 1 heart and up to 50 points. Shields and Magic Guards block it.

COINS & GEMS Every 10 coins in a row raises your multiplier (up to x5). Gems give +100.

POWER-UPS Shield, Magnet, 2X Coins, Heart, Star Turbo, Magic Fruit, Sky Lift and Antigravity.

CONTROLS Settings offer a fixed stick, tilt steering, left-handed layout and gamepad support. Keyboard: A/D, W, S, Space, Esc.
