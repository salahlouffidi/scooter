# Store listing draft

Title: SKY SCOOTER — LOUFFI EDITION

Short description:
Ride, jump and glide through 16 sky worlds in a scooter arcade adventure.

Full description:
Take your scooter into the skies. Steer through colorful worlds, dodge obstacles, collect coins and unlock the next challenge.

Explore 16 worlds with distinct scenery. Jump across gaps, brake through tight sections and use power-ups including shields, magnets and antigravity. Beat each world to unlock the next one, improve your best scores and try different scooter looks.

Choose floating or fixed touch controls, tilt steering or gamepad support. Adjust graphics, music, sound effects and motion settings to suit your device.

The game contains ads. When available, you can choose to watch a rewarded ad for one revive with one heart per run. An interstitial may appear at Game Over.

LOUFFI EDITION.
