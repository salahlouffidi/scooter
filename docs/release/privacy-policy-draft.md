## Game data

The game stores settings, progress, scores, virtual coins, unlocks, active-run snapshots and adaptive-difficulty information on your device. Android backup may include app data according to your device settings. The reviewed game does not require an account or directly request your name, contacts, precise location, camera or microphone.

## Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to display interstitial and optional rewarded advertisements. Google's advertising SDK may collect and share information such as IP address (including approximate location inferred from it), device and advertising identifiers, ad interactions, and diagnostic/performance information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Processing and retention depend on Google's services and your applicable choices.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
Google advertising information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

## Your controls

You can reset progress using the game's available reset controls. Clearing app storage in Android settings removes local app data, including saved runs and preferences; restored Android backups may reintroduce saved data. Manage backup and advertising preferences in your device settings. Requests concerning developer-held information can be sent to the privacy contact. Google provides its own controls for data processed by its services.

## Security and retention

Local game information remains until reset, cleared or otherwise removed, subject to Android backup behavior. Third-party service retention is governed by the provider's policies. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

## Updates

The developer may update this policy when app features or data processing change. The published policy will identify its effective date.
